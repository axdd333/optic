Cookie Policy

This Policy explains how Optic uses cookies, local storage, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies on the website and in the app.

Effective date

May 4, 2026

Contact

hello@tryoptic.app

1. What These Technologies Do

Cookies and similar technologies help services recognize browsers, keep sessions active, remember settings, protect accounts, measure performance, and understand usage.

2. Technologies Optic Uses

  • Strictly necessary technologies for login, sessions, security, fraud prevention, consent records, and service operation.
  • Preference technologies for language, display, region, interface choices, and app settings.
  • Analytics technologies for page views, feature use, diagnostics, performance, reliability, and crash reporting.
  • Support technologies for help-center, support chat, or support ticket state if enabled.

3. No Targeted Advertising Cookies

Optic does not use retargeting pixels, targeted advertising cookies, cross-context behavioral advertising cookies, or ad audience tools for the MVP.

If that changes, Optic must update this Policy, update the Privacy Policy, deploy appropriate preference controls, and obtain consent where required before activating those technologies.

4. Consent

Where required by law, Optic will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies. Strictly necessary technologies may operate without consent when needed to provide the Services.

5. Controls

You may manage these technologies through:

  • Optic cookie or privacy controls, where available;
  • browser settings;
  • device settings;
  • app permissions;
  • platform-level privacy controls;
  • legally required opt-out signals.

Blocking cookies or SDK access may affect account, subscription, security, analytics, or preference features.
