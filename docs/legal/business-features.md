# Optic Business Features Terms

Effective date: May 4, 2026

These lightweight terms apply only if Optic enables business, team, or
professional features before a full enterprise agreement exists.

## 1. Scope

Business features may include team billing, shared reports, professional
workflows, organization settings, or customer support for professional use.
These terms do not create a full enterprise data-processing program.

## 2. Authority

If you use Optic for an organization, you represent that you have authority
to use the Services for that organization and to provide any submitted data.

## 3. Additional Terms

Optic may require an order form, data-processing terms, security addendum, or
other agreement before enabling business features. Those additional terms
control if they conflict with these terms.

## 4. No Regulated Workflows

Business features are not designed for HIPAA, school records, marketplace
seller data, financial eligibility, employment screening, housing screening,
insurance underwriting, or public-benefit determinations.

## 5. Customer Data

Customers remain responsible for lawfully collecting, disclosing, and using
data they submit to Optic. Customers must not submit regulated sensitive data
unless Optic expressly supports the workflow in writing.

## 6. Future DPA

If Optic launches real B2B customer workspaces, admin controls, or customer
end-user processing, Optic should publish a Data Processing Addendum before
or alongside that launch.
