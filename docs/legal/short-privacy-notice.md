# Optic Short Privacy Notice

Effective date: May 4, 2026

Use this short notice at signup, checkout, trial start, account creation,
import flows, and any form that collects personal information.

## Default Notice

Optic collects your account details, subscription status, device and usage
data, support messages, and any data you choose to submit or authorize so we
can provide the service, generate private AI outputs and inferences, secure
accounts, process subscriptions, improve reliability, and comply with law.
Optic uses analytics only for the MVP and does not use targeted advertising.
Optic is not designed for minors or regulated sensitive data such as health,
biometric, financial, precise geolocation, or school-record data. Depending
on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export,
restrict, object to, or appeal certain processing. Read the Privacy Policy,
Cookie Policy, and Terms, or contact `hello@tryoptic.app`.

## Subscription Notice

By subscribing, you authorize recurring charges until you cancel. Trial
terms, renewal terms, price, billing frequency, and cancellation methods are
shown before purchase and in your receipt.

## Import Notice

Only import data you have the right to provide. Do not import health,
biometric, financial, precise geolocation, school-record, child, or similarly
regulated sensitive data unless Optic expressly supports that workflow.

## AI Output Notice

Optic outputs are probabilistic and may be wrong, incomplete, outdated, or
biased. They are private informational outputs only and are not professional
advice or eligibility determinations.
