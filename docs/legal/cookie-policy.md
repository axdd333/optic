# Optic Cookie Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Optic uses cookies, local storage, pixels,
SDKs, and similar technologies in connection with the Services.

## 1. What Cookies Do

Cookies and similar technologies help services recognize browsers, keep
sessions active, remember settings, protect accounts, measure performance,
and understand usage.

## 2. Cookies Optic Uses

Optic may use:

- strictly necessary cookies for login, sessions, security, fraud
  prevention, consent records, and service operation;
- preference cookies for language, display, region, and interface choices;
- analytics cookies for page views, feature use, diagnostics, performance,
  and reliability;
- support cookies for support chat or help-center state, if enabled.

## 3. No Targeted Advertising Cookies

Optic does not use retargeting pixels, targeted advertising cookies,
cross-context behavioral advertising cookies, or ad audience tools for the
MVP.

If that changes, Optic must update this Cookie Policy, update the Privacy
Policy, deploy appropriate preference controls, and obtain consent where
required before activating those technologies.

## 4. Consent

Where required by law, Optic will request consent before using non-essential
cookies. Strictly necessary cookies may operate without consent when needed
to provide the Services.

## 5. Cookie Controls

You may manage cookies through:

- Optic cookie controls, where available;
- browser settings;
- device settings;
- platform-level privacy controls;
- legally required opt-out signals.

Blocking cookies may affect account, subscription, security, or preference
features.

## 6. Cookie Inventory

The MVP cookie inventory must remain analytics-only.

| Cookie or tool | Provider | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| session | Optic | Session continuity | Necessary | Session |
| csrf | Optic | Request security | Necessary | Session |
| consent | Optic | Privacy choice record | Necessary | 12 months |
| analytics | Optic or vendor | Usage analytics | Analytics | 13 months |
| support | Optic or vendor | Support state | Functional | 30 days |

## 7. Changes

Optic may update this Cookie Policy as its cookie inventory changes.
