# Optic Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Optic collects, uses, discloses, and
protects personal information in connection with the Services.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies when Optic processes personal information for its own
purposes, including websites, accounts, subscriptions, trials, support,
analytics, AI inference features, and consumer Services.

Business, team, or enterprise features may be subject to additional terms.
Optic does not provide a full controller-processor enterprise program for
the MVP unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Optic, we may collect:

- identifiers, such as name, email, account ID, and contact details;
- account data, such as login state, settings, preferences, and plan;
- subscription data, such as purchase, renewal, trial, and payment status;
- device and usage data, such as IP address, browser, OS, page views,
  feature events, crash data, diagnostics, and approximate location from IP;
- communications, such as support requests, feedback, and survey responses;
- User Content, such as prompts, files, links, text, documents, public
  profile data, imported data, and other material you submit or authorize;
- AI inference data, such as generated summaries, profile signals,
  classifications, recommendations, confidence levels, and report history;
- cookie data, such as session IDs, preference records, and analytics IDs.

## 3. Sensitive Data

Optic is not designed for health, biometric, financial, precise geolocation,
school-record, child, or similarly regulated sensitive data. Do not provide
those data types unless Optic expressly supports the workflow.

If sensitive data appears in material you choose to import, Optic processes
it only to provide the requested feature, secure the Services, comply with
law, or honor your instructions.

## 4. Sources

We collect personal information:

- directly from you;
- from your device and browser;
- from subscriptions, app stores, and payment providers;
- from integrations or data sources you authorize;
- from public sources you ask Optic to analyze;
- from service providers that support the Services.

## 5. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to:

- provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Services;
- create and manage accounts, trials, subscriptions, and billing;
- authenticate users and prevent abuse;
- generate private outputs, reports, inferences, and recommendations;
- provide support and respond to requests;
- measure performance and usage through analytics;
- debug, test, improve, and develop the Services;
- communicate service, legal, security, billing, and marketing messages;
- comply with law and enforce terms;
- protect rights, safety, security, and service integrity.

## 6. AI, Inferences, And Training

Optic generates probabilistic outputs and inferences from data you provide,
authorize, or ask Optic to analyze. These outputs may be inaccurate,
incomplete, outdated, biased, or misunderstood.

For consumer Services, Optic may use interactions, feedback, diagnostics,
outputs, and User Content to evaluate, secure, improve, and develop the
Services, including AI models and inference systems. Optic will provide
controls where required by law or product design.

Business features may include different commitments in later terms.

## 7. Analytics And Advertising

Optic uses analytics to understand performance, reliability, feature usage,
and product quality. Optic does not use retargeting, ad pixels, targeted
advertising, or cross-context behavioral advertising for the MVP.

Optic does not sell personal information for money. Optic does not share
personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If Optic later adds targeted advertising, this Policy and the Cookie Policy
must be updated before the technology is deployed.

## 8. Disclosures

We may disclose personal information to:

- service providers, such as hosting, analytics, support, security, email,
  billing, and infrastructure providers;
- payment processors and app stores;
- integrations you connect or direct;
- professional advisors, auditors, insurers, and financing partners;
- law enforcement, regulators, courts, or others when required by law;
- parties involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, restructuring, or
  asset transfer;
- others with your consent or direction.

We may disclose aggregated or deidentified data that cannot reasonably
identify you.

## 9. Cookies

Optic uses cookies and similar technologies for essential functions,
security, preferences, and analytics. See the Cookie Policy for details.
Where required, Optic will request consent before using non-essential
cookies.

## 10. Retention

Optic retains personal information for as long as reasonably needed to
provide the Services, maintain accounts, honor choices, comply with law,
resolve disputes, enforce terms, prevent abuse, and keep business records.

Retention depends on data type, account status, subscription state, product
settings, legal requirements, and security needs. Where feasible, Optic
deletes or deidentifies personal information when it is no longer needed.

## 11. Security

Optic uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards
designed to protect personal information. No transmission or storage system
is completely secure.

## 12. International Transfers

Optic is operated from the United States and may process personal information
in the United States and other countries. Where required, Optic uses
appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as Standard
Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

## 13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

- access personal information;
- correct inaccurate personal information;
- delete personal information;
- receive a portable copy;
- restrict or object to processing;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising;
- appeal a denied privacy request;
- complain to a data protection authority.

Submit requests to `hello@tryoptic.app`. Optic may verify your identity before
responding and may deny or limit requests where permitted by law.

## 14. EEA, UK, And Swiss Users

For EEA, UK, and Swiss users, Optic's legal bases may include contract,
legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligation. Legitimate interests
include service operation, security, fraud prevention, analytics, product
improvement, and support.

You may contact Optic at `hello@tryoptic.app` to exercise rights or raise a
privacy concern. You may also contact your local supervisory authority.

## 15. U.S. State Privacy Rights

Residents of certain U.S. states may have rights to access, correct, delete,
port, appeal, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or profiling in
furtherance of significant decisions.

Optic does not use personal information for targeted advertising, sale, or
eligibility decisions for the MVP.

## 16. Children

Optic is not designed for minors and does not knowingly collect personal
information from users under 18. If you believe a minor has provided
personal information, contact `hello@tryoptic.app`.

## 17. Changes

Optic may update this Policy from time to time. If changes materially affect
privacy rights, Optic will provide reasonable notice through the Services,
email, or another appropriate method.

## 18. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to `hello@tryoptic.app`.
