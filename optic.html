why

A mirror with receipts. Not another score.

Digital life now produces a second self: email patterns, calendar behavior, public profiles, documents, metadata, search results, and old context that still gets read as current truth.

Optic turns authorized sources into a private feedback loop: what signals exist, what they may imply, where perception diverges from intent, and which repair actions are worth taking first.

trust

Private feedback, not public judgment.

Optic is not surveillance, not a public score, and not a system for institutions to use against people. It is a user-side product for self-understanding, reputation repair, and social calibration.

Local-first Raw personal data stays on device.

User-owned People control what is shared.

Explicit boundary No public score, buyer dashboard, or institutional feed.

Optional calibration Privacy-preserving contribution can improve the system over time.
